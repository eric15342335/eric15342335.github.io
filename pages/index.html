About Me

I am currently a Year 4 undergraduate student at the University of Hong Kong, studying Applied Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science. I am spending a semester at Fudan University in Shanghai, China, studying Computer Science and Technology as an exchange student under the HKU Worldwide Undergraduate Student Exchange Programme (HKUWW). Previously, I worked as a summer intern at FWD Group Management Holdings Limited under Group Technology and Digital (Group Technology Services), where I worked with colleagues in the Finance Cloud Infrastructure and Operations team.

I am passionate about artificial intelligence technologies (automatic machine learning frameworks, computer vision, LLMs, model inference and deployment), cloud computing (like AWS, which I am currently using), and DevOps (CI/CD, IaC, large-scale distributed system management). The order of these interests does not indicate my proficiency or level of interest (in that case, you might stalk my GitHub starred repositories to better know what I really like!).

I am an open-source enthusiast. As a former member of the PyInstaller development team, I actively contributed hook patch requests to the pyinstaller-hooks-contrib repository in 2021, collaborating with developers such as bwoodsend and rokm.

For more information, please refer to my curriculum vitae. It is written in LaTeX, and you can download the code from GitHub and compile it yourself if you love my CV style.
